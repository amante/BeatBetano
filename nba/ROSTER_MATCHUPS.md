# NBA roster, rotations and matchup evidence — PR #44

The single pregame workflow now produces **roster_matchups.json** for every NBA game, covering both teams. It records any supplementary ESPN player injury observations separately, then checks whether a reliable pre-tipoff source confirms the five starters, injury report, bench players and projected minutes, plus a sourced and timely matchup evaluation.

Optional verified data can be passed to `nba/roster_matchups.py --verified-data path.json`, keyed under `games[game_id].home`, `games[game_id].away`, and `games[game_id].matchup`. Each team must include `lineup_confirmed`, five unique `confirmed_starters`, `lineup_source`, `official_injury_report_verified`, `official_injuries`, `injury_source`, `rotation_verified`, `bench_rotation` with player and expected minutes, and `source_observed_at_utc`. The matchup must have `verified`, `source`, `source_observed_at_utc`, and optional notes.

Crucial limitations: **these are validation/schema checks, NOT authoritative NBA data ingestion**. Presently no official quintet source, NBA injury cross-check or rotation projection is automatically populated. Flags supplied by a user are not independently authenticated and are not evidence of calibration. No numerical lineup/injury adjustment is created. The integrated probability remains null until the full seven-module evidence and calibrated sport-impact model are actually connected. Do not represent a successful workflow as complete game analysis, and never use bookmaker odds as sports model features. No NFL changes.
